Science notes & sources ↗

This is a fictional scenario. Values, costs, results and windows are game approximations, not NASA predictions. New observations can raise or lower impact probability; they do not always reduce it. NASA uses follow-up observations to refine orbits, infrared to estimate sizes, and radar to study suitable nearby objects. Our shared appointment, team availability, task durations, and radar opening time are simplified game rules. Amount of matter here is inferred from size and assumed density, not directly measured. Outside surface details and a specific impact point need closer observations.
